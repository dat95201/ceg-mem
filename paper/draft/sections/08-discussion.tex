\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The two halves of typed memory fail and succeed for the same reason. Obeying
\cref{eq:steer} would require the proposer to simulate the oracle's verdict on
a class it has only been told about in the abstract, which is harder than
writing a patch; the guard needs no such simulation, because it re-executes a
concrete counterexample and reads the answer. That is why the model
\emph{conditions} on the exclusion block---trajectories diverge the moment it
appears---while re-drawing the excluded classes at an unchanged rate, and why
the concurrent text-to-SQL work~\cite{vo2026merit} reports the same
non-separation. A class label is worth more as an \emph{index over evidence}
than as an \emph{instruction to a model}; the design consequence is to count
oracle calls and executions rather than tokens, since the resource identified
as squandered~\cite{lin2026run} is invisible in a token budget.

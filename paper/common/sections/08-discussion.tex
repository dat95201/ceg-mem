\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The two halves of typed memory depend on different things, and that is why one
transfers across proposers and the other does not. Obeying \cref{eq:steer}
requires the proposer to simulate the oracle's verdict on a class described to
it only in the abstract, which is harder than writing a patch and is a
capability the proposer either has or lacks; the guard needs no such
simulation, since it re-executes a concrete counterexample and reads the
answer, and so owes nothing to the proposer. Hence the 7B model
\emph{conditions} on the exclusion block---trajectories diverge the moment it
appears---while re-drawing the excluded classes at an unchanged rate, as the
concurrent text-to-SQL work~\cite{vo2026merit} also reports; and hence
\texttt{gpt-4o-mini}, which can act on the block, converts it into repair
(\cref{sec:results-scale}). A class label is worth more as an \emph{index over
evidence} than as an \emph{instruction to a model} unless the model is one that
follows instructions of that kind; the design consequence is to count oracle
calls and executions rather than tokens, since the resource identified as
squandered~\cite{lin2026run} is invisible in a token budget.

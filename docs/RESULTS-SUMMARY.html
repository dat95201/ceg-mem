Where the claims stand

Six formal claims, scored against 35,755 logged rounds after five measurement defects were fixed. Four hold. One is partly established and structurally blocked from showing more. One is not yet testable — and every one of those moves came from fixing how a number was computed, not from new data.

29 Aug 2026 branch fixed-pipeline 106 tasks · 5 bands · 530 cells/arm 3,217 episodes frozen

Scored on sandbox_runs — program executions, the unit that charges the guard for its own work — not on oracle calls. E9 has not run, so Cor. 4.4 stays open by construction. E6-transcript never ran, so every transcript figure below rests on a single episode and is excluded.

Five measurement defects, no new data. The cost unit ignored the guard’s own sandbox executions. The redundancy count summed a repeat caught with one missed, over a reference set that shrank in whichever arm guarded more. NCDR divided by a denominator that could never reach its own numerator. FSRR’s denominator, once the censoring was repaired, put a rate above 1.0. And typing_random never reached the frozen episode, so E5-random’s 90 episodes passed every main-grid filter and pooled into typed — that arm carried 620 episodes against untyped’s 530. All five are fixed. Every figure on this page is post-fix, and none of them came from new data.

Unclaimed

Two results the theory never predicted

Both are cleaner than anything in the table above, and neither depends on E9.

1. The saving has a gradient

Memory versus no memory, in program executions. A flat 2.5× had no mechanism; a saving that appears exactly where the search is hard and vanishes where it is easy does.

2. Oracle strength sets the overfitting rate

E4 varied how many counterexamples the oracle may try. Every accepted patch was then re-scored on the held-out half of the shipped pool. The result is a dose–response curve over 1,368 audited patches that reaches zero.

The 0.476 figure was being read as a defect. It is the worst point of a curve, at an oracle deliberately crippled to three examples. At full strength the rate is 0 of 1,146, with correct_over_plausible = 1.0 in both memory arms. Long & Rinard (ICSE 2016) put the base rate for repair search spaces at hundreds of plausible patches per correct one; AtCoder's dense suites are the other extreme, and this quantifies by how much.

Caveats

What would weaken all of this

  • The guard is not deterministic. 3 of 3,170 rounds show the typed guard blocking where the flat scan did not, which set inclusion forbids. _still_refutes counts a wall-clock timeout as a refutation, so the guard is a function of machine load. 0.09%, but the artifact does not reproduce bit-for-bit.
  • The pairing is thrown away. The design is paired by common random numbers and analysed as if it were not. Wilcoxon signed-rank, not Mann–Whitney; and every “no difference” sentence is an equivalence claim needing a TOST at a declared margin.
  • Two falsifiers now pass that could not before. no_memory and untyped draw byte-identical patches under common random numbers, so they must agree exactly on any property of the draw sequence — and they now do, on redundancy present (RR 1.000, 530/530 cells), on FSRR (0.388) and on type entropy (1.695 bits). The typed arm’s FSRR is 0.179, and that 54% gap, no longer confounded with censoring, is the steering effect isolated.
  • The typed arm still needs E8 to be fully measurable. Its exclusion block changes the prompt, so only 13.6% of its censored rounds pair with a no-memory draw. On E8-audit’s own universe — 30 sweep tasks, seeds 1–3 — coverage is complete and all four arms agree on redundancy present (4.68), with paid redundancy 4.68 / 0.04 / 0.13 / 0.12 for none / flat / guard-only / typed. Outside it, typed’s redundancy is under-counted and must not be quoted.
  • NCDR has no prior art. No published metric by that name or definition, so it carries no construct-validity argument by inheritance.
  • Contamination is unaddressed. ConDefects spans Oct 2021 to Sep 2023 by the paper and to Jun 2024 by the repo; any 2024+ open-weight model's cutoff encloses that window.
  • No transcript baseline. E6 never ran — one episode is in the log and it is excluded from every figure here. A reviewer will ask how this compares to ChatRepair.
